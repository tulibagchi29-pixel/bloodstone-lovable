# Restructure the Bloodstone Homepage

## Goal
Lead with *Bloodstone* as a rigorous hard-science-fiction novel, explain its distinctive scientific ideas without exposing plot turns, and reserve the Cixin Liu comparison as supporting context near the end.

## Homepage flow
1. **Opening** — Reframe the first screen around “hard science fiction rooted in real mathematics,” while retaining the book cover, author/artist credit, and order buttons.
2. **Premise** — Keep the existing jacket copy early, with light edits only where needed to preserve mystery.
3. **The science inside Bloodstone** — Add a visually strong editorial section that assimilates the supplied concepts into concise, spoiler-light thematic entries:
   - myth as measurable geomagnetism
   - mathematics as transmission
   - consciousness as pattern
   - individuality and consent as physical variables
   - collective behavior as synchronization
   - mathematical history as partial reception
   - first contact as a network event
   - transcendence as an engineering risk
   - oral knowledge outlasting official records
4. **Worldwide availability** — Keep the international Amazon links prominent after the science section so interest leads directly to purchase.
5. **Creators** — Keep author and illustrator context before the comparison.
6. **Comparison at the end** — Move “Why the comparison holds” near the closing purchase section and frame Liu Cixin as a useful point of reference rather than the homepage’s primary identity.
7. **Closing order prompt and footer** — Preserve Amazon and Notion Press links.
